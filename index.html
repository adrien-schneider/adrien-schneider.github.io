About

I am a Research Engineer and PhD candidate working on Speech Processing and AI at Université Grenoble Alpes, at the LIG laboratory, under the supervision of Prof. Dominique Vaufreydaz and Associate Professor Frédérique Letué.

My research focuses on AI-based voice anonymization and speech privacy. I am interested in developing methods that protect speaker identity while preserving the linguistic and paralinguistic information contained in speech. My work explores speech representation learning and the evaluation of privacy–utility trade-offs, with a particular interest in real-world and multi-speaker speech.

Research interests: Voice anonymization · Speech privacy · Speech representation learning · Speech processing
